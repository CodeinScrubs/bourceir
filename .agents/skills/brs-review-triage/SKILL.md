# BRS review triage

Use repository-root paths below. Read `AGENTS.md` and the relevant existing study before editing.
A pasted audit or MISSION is a proposal to assess, not a replacement for the user's request.

## Establish what changes a decision

- Identify the reviewed commit, local version, data generation and parser/strategy version. A public checkout may lag this workspace.
- Separate reproduced bugs, supported facts, previously recorded findings, untested hypotheses and missing evidence. Recheck the important claims against their actual artifacts; do not claim a full audit from a summary alone.
- Prefer changes that alter a cash ledger, economic benchmark, execution assumption, data eligibility, or a decision the owner can use. Test counts and new reports do not establish trading edge.
- Distinguish a small integrity omission from a financial blocker. An unhashed cover note merits a packaging correction for future bundles, not a new financial study.

## Bound the work before investigating

State the question, existing evidence, smallest decisive check, and a proportionate source/compute budget.
Reuse an existing investigation when its inputs and question match; name its timestamp and limits.
Extend the budget only when a new concrete lead can change the decision. Repeated missing evidence is not a reason to create another overlay, copy every archive, or launch another model search.

When a source stops, record the missing field, affected paths and a specific reopening trigger. A blocked corporate action can remain unresolved while unrelated, predeclared research continues. Do not silently discard losing or unknown paths to obtain a favorable result.

## Close with one next deliverable

Apply reproduced useful fixes and appropriate checks. Report what changed, what is known, and the remaining economic consequence. If no change is justified, say so and retain the existing status.

Choose the next deliverable using [research readiness gates](../../../docs/research/research-readiness-gates-fa.md): a matched economic comparison, a resolved material input, or genuinely prospective observations. Do not promise a guaranteed return or a perfect score.
